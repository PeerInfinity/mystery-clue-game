THE AFFAIR OF THE COUNTING HOUSE

I am Penn, clerk to Mr. Arkwright, and on the Monday night I found him dead in the strongroom.

The door of the strongroom was bolted on the inside.

The chimney of the strongroom was barred with iron.

The walls of the strongroom were sound.

Pipe smoke from the office drifted into the strongroom that night.

Old Silas keeps colonial animals in the office.

A small grating in the wall opened into the office.

A chain hung from a hook beside the grating.

The chain reached down to the ledger desk, which was bolted to the floor.

Arkwright had been bitten, and it was a ferret that had come down the chain.
