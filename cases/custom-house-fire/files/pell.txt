WHAT MR. PELL TOLD ME

Mr. Josiah Pell, the Collector, at Cliff House on Wednesday afternoon. I wrote it down as he spoke.

"I locked the street door at six on Tuesday, and my key was on my watch-chain all night."

"The evening was mild, and I left my caped cloak on its peg in the lobby."

"I opened the Board's letter on Monday, and I told nobody of it but Mr. Prout."

"On Tuesday, after Mr. Prout had gone at half past five, I put the bond ledger in my bag to cast the brandy account at home. I told nobody that I had taken it."
