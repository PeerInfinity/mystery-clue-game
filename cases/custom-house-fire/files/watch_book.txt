THE NIGHT WATCH BOOK

The book of the night watch of the quay, kept in the watch-box. Its printed heading: the watch is to try the Custom House door and sign this book every hour, from ten until six.

Tuesday night. 10. C.H. door tried, fast. S. Crouch.

11. C.H. door tried, fast. S. Crouch.

12. C.H. door tried, fast. S. Crouch.

2. Custom House afire. S. Crouch.

3. S. Crouch.

4. S. Crouch.

5. S. Crouch.

6. S. Crouch.
