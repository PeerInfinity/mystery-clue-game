THE QUAY GATE-KEEPER'S NIGHT BOOK

The night book of the keeper of the quay gate.

3 September, 11 at night. Marsh's cart, casks, on a pass. Out by the town gate, up Mill Lane.

24 September, 11 at night. Marsh's cart, casks, on a pass. Out by the town gate, up Mill Lane.

8 October, 11 at night. Marsh's cart, casks, on a pass. Out by the town gate, up Mill Lane.

Where did Marsh's cart take the casks: to Mr. Garrick's cellar in Mill Lane, or to the Marigold's berth?
