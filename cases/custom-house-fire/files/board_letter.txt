THE BOARD'S LETTER TO THE COLLECTOR

From the Board of Customs, London, to the Collector at Saltmere, in confidence. Dated Friday the tenth of October; by its postmark it was delivered at Saltmere on Monday the thirteenth. I had a copy of it with me.

Our Surveyor, Mr. Ashby, will attend at Saltmere on Thursday the sixteenth to examine the bond ledger and to compare every export entry with the vessels' papers.
