WHAT THE BAKER TOLD ME

Tobias Hearn, baker, at his oven door in Quay Street on Thursday.

"Some minutes before the bell a man in a caped cloak came out of the Custom House door, pulled it to, and went off toward the hard."

"It was the Collector. I knew him by the cloak. I did not see his face."

Who came out of the Custom House in the Collector's cloak: Mr. Pell, Mr. Prout or Silas Crouch?
