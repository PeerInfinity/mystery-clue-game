THE RAILWAY TIMETABLE

The printed timetable of the Saltmere branch. By its heading, Tormouth is fifty-eight miles from Saltmere by road.

Saltmere 7.40 p.m., arriving Tormouth 9.10. The last train back leaves Tormouth at 9.30 and reaches Saltmere at 11.00. The first in the morning leaves Tormouth at 6.35 and reaches Saltmere at 8.05.
