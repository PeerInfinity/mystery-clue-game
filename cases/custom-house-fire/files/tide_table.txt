THE TIDE TABLE

The printed tide table for Saltmere. Its note: the bar carries water for vessels and boats only from two hours before high water until two hours after.

24 September: high water 4.40 a.m. and 5.05 p.m.; low water 10.50 a.m.

14 October: high water 9.30 p.m. 15 October: low water 3.40 a.m.; high water 9.55 a.m.
