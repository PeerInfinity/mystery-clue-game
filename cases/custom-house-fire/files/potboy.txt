WHAT THE POTBOY TOLD ME

Peter Lobb, potboy at the Anchor, on Thursday.

"As the taproom clock struck a quarter to one a man spoke low to me from the dark of the yard door, and gave me twopence to run to the watch-box. I was to say: Mr. Odell wants Crouch at the pier-head lamp directly, it wants oil. I never saw the man's face."

"I told Crouch, and he took his lantern and went off down the mole."

"When the bell rang there were only my master, old Tully and me in the house. Mr. Prout's chair was empty."

Who sent the boy to call Crouch away: Mr. Prout, Mr. Pell or Mr. Garrick?
