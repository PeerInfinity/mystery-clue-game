THE BOND LEDGER: THE MARIGOLD ENTRIES

The bond ledger of the Custom House at Saltmere, Mr. Garrick's brandy account. Three entries name the Marigold.

3 September: 12 casks delivered for export, per brig Marigold, Venn master, lying at the New Quay berth. Was the Marigold at Saltmere on the third of September? And was this entry written on that day?

24 September: 12 casks for export per Marigold; cleared outwards and sailed at noon. Could the Marigold have sailed at noon on the twenty-fourth of September?

8 October: 16 casks for export per Marigold.

What are these three entries: true exports, a clerk's honest slips of date, or false?
