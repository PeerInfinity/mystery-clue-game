WHAT THE LANDLORD OF THE ANCHOR TOLD ME

Mr. Bales, landlord of the Anchor, in his taproom on Thursday.

"Mr. Prout sat at cribbage from ten, and he paid and left at a quarter past one by the taproom clock."

"When the bell rang Mr. Prout was not in the house."

How long had Mr. Prout been gone from the Anchor when the bell rang: half an hour, or no time at all?
