THREE PASSES FROM THE GATE-KEEPER'S FILE

Three slips from the file of the quay gate-keeper. All three are in one hand, and each is headed "Custom House, Saltmere" with its date.

3 September. Pass Marsh's cart with 12 casks from the bond store. J.P.

24 September. Pass Marsh's cart with 12 casks from the bond store. J.P.

8 October. Pass Marsh's cart with 16 casks from the bond store. J.P.

Whose initials are the J.P. on these passes: Josiah Pell's, or Jonas Prout's? And who in the Custom House let the brandy out of bond: Mr. Pell, or Mr. Prout?
