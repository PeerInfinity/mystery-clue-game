THE RENT BOX, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Ada Orme, the keeper of the lodging-house at 9 Tanner's Row. Mr. Fenn lives at 9 Tanner's Row. Captain Rudd lives at 9 Tanner's Row. Nobody but me, Mr. Fenn and Captain Rudd lived at 9 Tanner's Row. The rent box was in the dining-room. At one o'clock on Tuesday, the week's rents were in the rent box. At three o'clock on Tuesday, the week's rents were not in the rent box. Between one o'clock on Tuesday and three o'clock on Tuesday, somebody took the week's rents from 9 Tanner's Row. Who took the week's rents?

From one o'clock on Tuesday until four o'clock on Tuesday, Captain Rudd was at 9 Tanner's Row. Captain Rudd told me: "At half past two on Tuesday, Mr. Fenn was in the yard. The back door was open."

At four o'clock on Tuesday, Mr. Fenn knocked at the front door. Mr. Fenn told me: "At half past two on Tuesday, I was at the reading room in Cable Street."

Which lodger told Ada Orme a thing that was not so?

At one o'clock on Tuesday, Mr. Fenn went out by the front door. From one o'clock on Tuesday until four o'clock on Tuesday, the front door was locked. I had the key of the front door. From one o'clock on Tuesday until three o'clock on Tuesday, I was in the kitchen. The back door was shut. I saw this from the kitchen.

Later on Tuesday, the week's rents were in Captain Rudd's sea-chest. Captain Rudd said to the constable: "It was I who took the week's rents from 9 Tanner's Row." Captain Rudd said to the constable: "It was I who told Ada Orme a thing that was not so."
