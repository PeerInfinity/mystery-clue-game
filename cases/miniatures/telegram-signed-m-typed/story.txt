THE TELEGRAM SIGNED M., TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Dr. Ellis, a doctor, of Thornby. Mr. Joseph Holt is the owner of Dyer's Mill. At ten o'clock on Saturday night, Mr. Joseph Holt was at Dyer's Mill. At eleven o'clock on Saturday night, Mr. Joseph Holt was struck down.

At eleven o'clock on Saturday night, Mr. Joseph Holt had the telegram. Who sent the telegram? Who was waiting for Mr. Joseph Holt at Dyer's Mill?

Mary Holt is Mr. Joseph Holt's wife. Mr. Martin Grove is Mr. Joseph Holt's partner in Dyer's Mill.

From three o'clock on Saturday until five o'clock on Saturday, Mary Holt was at the church fête. I was at the church fête.

Mr. Grove's clerk told me: "At two o'clock on Saturday, Mr. Martin Grove took the train to Leeds."

At half past nine on Saturday night, Mr. Martin Grove drew the key of Dyer's Mill. From half past nine on Saturday night until ten o'clock on Saturday night, nobody but Mr. Martin Grove had a key of Dyer's Mill.

On Monday, Mr. Martin Grove said to me: "It was I who handed in the telegram at the post office at Leeds." Mr. Martin Grove said to me: "It was I who waited at Dyer's Mill."
