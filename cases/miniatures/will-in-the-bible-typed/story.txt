THE WILL IN THE BIBLE, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Henry Dacre, solicitor, of Wells. On the second of May, Miss Harriet Pym died. Three years before, the earlier will gave Ashcombe House to Mrs. Grice and the rest of the estate to the church. Mrs. Grice is the housekeeper of Miss Harriet Pym.

In August, Mr. Lionel Pym brought the later will to me. Mr. Lionel Pym told me: "In August, I found the later will in the family Bible." Somebody made the later will. Was the later will made when it is dated? Who made the later will?

The family Bible was in the sitting-room of Ashcombe House. From the second of May until August, Mr. Lionel Pym had the keys of Ashcombe House. From the sixth of May until August, Mrs. Grice was at Frome.

On the fifth of May, Miss Harriet Pym was buried. On the fourteenth of June, Thomas Pym was born, the son of Mr. Lionel Pym. On the first of July, Thomas Pym was christened.

In September, Mr. Lionel Pym said to me: "It was I who made the later will." Mr. Lionel Pym said to me: "On the third of March, the later will was not made."
