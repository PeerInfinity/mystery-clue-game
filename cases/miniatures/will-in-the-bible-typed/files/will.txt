THE LATER WILL

In August, the later will was dated the third of March. The later will revoked the earlier will and gave all she had to Mr. Lionel Pym.

In August, the later will gave a silver watch to Thomas Pym, in these words: "To my great-nephew Thomas, whom I held at the font, I leave my mother's silver watch."

In August, the later will was in a hand very like the hand of Miss Harriet Pym.
