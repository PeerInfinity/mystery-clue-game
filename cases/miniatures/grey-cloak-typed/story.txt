THE GREY CLOAK, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Sergeant Tull, sergeant, of Wickham. On Wednesday morning, the cash drawer of the surgery was forced, with the week's takings gone.

Mrs. Lunn lives at the house opposite the side door of the surgery. Mrs. Lunn told me: "At nine o'clock on Tuesday night, the figure at the side door was at the side door of the surgery. The figure at the side door wore the long grey cloak of Dr. Mott. The figure at the side door was Dr. Mott. I knew it by this: the figure at the side door wore the long grey cloak of Dr. Mott." Who was the figure at the side door?

From eight o'clock on Tuesday night until ten o'clock on Tuesday night, Dr. Mott was at Squire Hale's house. I was at Squire Hale's house.

From eight o'clock on Tuesday night until ten o'clock on Tuesday night, Dr. Mott wore the greatcoat of Dr. Mott. On Wednesday morning, the long grey cloak of Dr. Mott was in the passage of the surgery. The long grey cloak of Dr. Mott was damp.

Nobody but Dr. Mott, Mr. Pell and Kitty had a key of the side door. Mr. Pell is the dispenser. Kitty is the housemaid. Kitty was short. Mrs. Lunn told me: "At nine o'clock on Tuesday night, the figure at the side door was tall."

On Wednesday afternoon, Mr. Pell said to me: "I was the figure at the side door."
