THE NIGHT PORTER'S STATEMENT, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

Inspector Reeve's note. Josiah Kemp is the night porter of Bly's Bank. On Friday morning, the strongroom of Bly's Bank was open. The four hundred pounds was not in the strongroom of Bly's Bank. Between ten o'clock on Thursday night and seven o'clock on Friday morning, somebody took the four hundred pounds from Bly's Bank. Who took the four hundred pounds?

Josiah Kemp's statement. From ten o'clock on Thursday night until seven o'clock on Friday morning, I was at Bly's Bank. At ten o'clock on Thursday night, Mr. Bly gave the keys of the bank and of the strongroom to me. From ten o'clock on Thursday night until seven o'clock on Friday morning, I had the keys of the bank and of the strongroom.

Tobias Finch is a clerk turned off from Bly's Bank last month. At one o'clock on Friday morning, Tobias Finch was at Bly's Bank.

Inspector Reeve's note. On Friday morning, the strongroom door was opened with its key, and not forced.

From Wednesday until seven o'clock on Friday morning, Tobias Finch was in the lock-up at Cross Street.

On Friday afternoon, the four hundred pounds was in the porter's box. Josiah Kemp said to me: "It was I who took the four hundred pounds from Bly's Bank."
