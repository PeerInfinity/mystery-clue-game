THE SAME MONDAY, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Ned Arliss, clerk to the harbour-master at Saltby. At six o'clock on Sunday, the chart of the new channel was at the harbour office. Captain Dunmore locked the office door. At nine o'clock on Monday, the chart of the new channel was not at the harbour office. Between six o'clock on Sunday and nine o'clock on Monday, somebody took the chart of the new channel from the harbour office. Who took the chart of the new channel from the harbour office?

I have three hours today, and more to do than they will hold. When they are spent I wake again on this same morning, with the day before me as it was. What I have learned I keep.

On Monday, before noon, Silas Finch said to me: "It was I who took the chart of the new channel from the harbour office."
