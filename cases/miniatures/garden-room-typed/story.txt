THE GARDEN ROOM, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Ellen Wray, the housekeeper of Holm Lodge. At four o'clock on Saturday, Mr. Tobias Wray's gold repeater was in the garden room. From four o'clock on Saturday until eight o'clock on Saturday, Mr. Tobias Wray was on the upper floor. At eight o'clock on Saturday, Mr. Tobias Wray's gold repeater was not in the garden room. Between four o'clock on Saturday and eight o'clock on Saturday, somebody took Mr. Tobias Wray's gold repeater from the garden room. Who took Mr. Tobias Wray's gold repeater from the garden room?

The garden room was fitted with two doors and no window that opens. Philip Wray is the nephew of Mr. Tobias Wray. Mr. Farrant is a neighbour. From four o'clock on Saturday until eight o'clock on Saturday, nobody but me, Mr. Tobias Wray, Philip Wray and Mr. Farrant was at Holm Lodge.

Dunn is the gardener. Dunn told me: "From four o'clock on Saturday until dusk on Saturday, I was on the terrace. The terrace door of the garden room was shut. I saw this from the terrace. From five o'clock on Saturday until dusk on Saturday, Mr. Farrant was on the terrace. From dusk on Saturday until eight o'clock on Saturday, I was not on the terrace."

From four o'clock on Saturday until eight o'clock on Saturday, I was in the passage. The passage door of the garden room was shut. I saw this from the passage. From four o'clock on Saturday until dusk on Saturday, Philip Wray was in the passage. From dusk on Saturday until eight o'clock on Saturday, Philip Wray was not in the passage. Mr. Farrant was in the passage.

On Monday, Mr. Tobias Wray's gold repeater was at the pawnbroker's in Castle Street. Philip Wray said to me: "It was I who took Mr. Tobias Wray's gold repeater from the garden room."
