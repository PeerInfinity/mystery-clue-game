THE RENT BOX PAPERS

I am Ada Orme, the keeper of the lodging-house at 9 Tanner's Row. Mr. Fenn lives there, and Captain Rudd too. Nobody but me and those two lived there. The rent box stood in the dining-room. At one o'clock on Tuesday, the week's rents were in the box. At three o'clock on Tuesday, they were not. Somebody took them from the house between one o'clock on Tuesday and three o'clock on Tuesday. Who took the rents?

Captain Rudd was at 9 Tanner's Row from one o'clock on Tuesday until four o'clock on Tuesday.

Mr. Fenn knocked at the front door at four o'clock on Tuesday.

Which lodger told me a thing that was not so?

Later on Tuesday, the week's rents were in Captain Rudd's sea-chest. Captain Rudd said to the constable: "It was I who took the rents from 9 Tanner's Row." He said to the constable too: "I was the one who told Ada Orme a thing that was not so."
