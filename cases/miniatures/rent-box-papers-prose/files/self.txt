MY OWN AFTERNOON

Mr. Fenn went out by the front door at one o'clock on Tuesday. From one o'clock on Tuesday until four o'clock on Tuesday, that door was locked. I had the key of it for the same hours.

I was in the kitchen from one o'clock on Tuesday until three o'clock on Tuesday. In those same hours, from one o'clock on Tuesday until three o'clock on Tuesday, the back door was shut. I saw this from the kitchen.
