MY OWN AFTERNOON

Mr. Fenn stood by with his hat on while I counted the rents. I let him out at the front door and locked it behind him, for the latch is broken, and the key did not leave my apron pocket until he knocked at four.

Then I went to the kitchen and was baking there until three o'clock, with the back door at my elbow. It is the only way in from the yard, and it did not open once.
