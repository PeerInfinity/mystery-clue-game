THE RENT BOX PAPERS

I am Ada Orme. I keep a lodging-house at 9 Tanner's Row, with two lodgers, Mr. Fenn and Captain Rudd, and no servant. On Tuesday at one o'clock I counted the week's rents into the box on the dining-room sideboard, four pounds and some silver. At three o'clock I went to the box for the milk money, and it was empty.

I went straight up to the Captain, and when he had told me where he had been, I wrote it down.

Mr. Fenn knocked at four o'clock, and I wrote down what he told me too.

Then I read the two over, and saw that one of my lodgers was telling me a thing that was not so. So I wrote down my own afternoon as well, as nearly as I could.

I sent for the constable and gave him my three papers. He found my four pounds in the Captain's sea-chest, and the Captain, when it was put to him, owned that he had seen nobody in the yard at all.
