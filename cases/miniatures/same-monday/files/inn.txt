AT THE SHIP INN

Mrs. Pask keeps the Ship, where the coach stops. "The Captain took the Marlow coach from my door at six on Sunday," she told me, "and he was at Marlow until the first coach brought him back at nine this morning. I handed him down myself."

"If it is the night you ask about," she said, "ask Jem Tolley, the night watchman. He lodges at the ropewalk and will be abed now, but he will wake for you."
