THE LAMP-KEEPER'S WORD, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Hale, the constable at Saltmarsh. The bonded store was on the quay. At about ten o'clock on Tuesday night, the bonded store was broken open. Tobias Wren is a boatman. On Wednesday morning, Tobias Wren was held to be the one who broke open the bonded store. Marta Quill is the keeper of the harbour lamp. At about ten o'clock on Tuesday night, Marta Quill was at the harbour lamp. Where did Marta Quill see Tobias Wren at about ten o'clock on Tuesday night?

Marta Quill was without a voice, and wrote what she had to say. I knew Marta Quill's hand.

Pike is the harbourmaster. Pike told me: "Marta Quill wrote to me: 'At about ten o'clock on Tuesday night, Tobias Wren was on the quay.'"

Elsie Quill is Marta Quill's niece. At noon on Wednesday, Elsie Quill had the basket. Elsie Quill told me: "Marta Quill wrote to me: 'At about ten o'clock on Tuesday night, Tobias Wren was at the chapel.'"

Who changed the word of Marta Quill?

Last spring, Pike came to blows with Tobias Wren. Elsie Quill is to be married to Tobias Wren at Michaelmas.

From noon on Wednesday until later on Thursday, the basket was at my door. On Thursday morning, the folded paper was in the basket. The folded paper was in Marta Quill's hand, with the name of Elsie Quill on it. The folded paper said: "Take this to the constable. At ten Wren was on the quay, under my lamp. M. Q."

Later on Thursday, Elsie Quill said to me: "At about ten o'clock on Tuesday night, Tobias Wren was on the quay." Elsie Quill said to me: "It was I who changed the word of Marta Quill."
