THE WILL IN THE BIBLE

I am Henry Dacre, solicitor, of Wells. Miss Harriet Pym of Ashcombe House died on the second of May. The will she made at my office three years before leaves the house to Mrs. Grice, her housekeeper, and the rest of her estate to the church.

In August her nephew, Mr. Lionel Pym, brought me a later will, which he said he had found that week in the family Bible. I copied it out at once. If it was made when it says, and by her, then the church and Mrs. Grice had nothing.

The Bible had lain in Miss Pym's sitting-room all her life. Since her death Mr. Pym, as her executor, had kept the keys of the house. Mrs. Grice went to her sister at Frome the day after the funeral, and had not been back.

I rode over to Ashcombe and read the parish register. Miss Pym was buried on the fifth of May. Thomas, son of Lionel Pym, was born on the fourteenth of June and christened on the first of July.

When I showed Mr. Pym the register he sat a long while, and then said he had only wanted to keep the house in the family. He had written the will himself, in her hand as near as he could make it.
