THE WILL FOUND IN THE BIBLE

"This is the last will of me, Harriet Pym, of Ashcombe House, made this third day of March. I revoke all former wills, and leave all I have to my nephew Lionel Pym."

"To my great-nephew Thomas, whom I held at the font, I leave my mother's silver watch."

The hand is very like hers, and the signature too.
