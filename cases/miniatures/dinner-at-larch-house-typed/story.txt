THE DINNER AT LARCH HOUSE, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

George Marlow's account. I am George Marlow, the master of Larch House. The drawer of the study desk was in the study. When the soup was served on Thursday, my notes were in the drawer of the study desk. When the guests had gone, my notes were not in the drawer of the study desk. Who took George Marlow's notes from the study?

From when the soup was served on Thursday until the guests had gone, I was in the dining-room. Hannah is the maid. From when the soup was served on Thursday until the guests had gone, Hannah was in the dining-room. Everyone but Clara Marlow, Captain Voss and Miss Pym was in the dining-room. I saw this from the dining-room. From when the soup was served on Thursday until the joint was carried in, Clara Marlow was on the upper floor. When the fish was served, Captain Voss was not in the dining-room. When the dessert was served, Miss Pym was not in the dining-room. From when the soup was served on Thursday until the joint was carried in, Miss Pym was in the dining-room. I saw this from the dining-room. From when the joint was carried in until the guests had gone, Captain Voss was in the dining-room. I saw this from the dining-room.

Clara Marlow's account. From when the soup was served on Thursday until the joint was carried in, I was on the nursery landing.

When the fish was served, Captain Voss was in the hall. From when the soup was served on Thursday until the joint was carried in, Captain Voss was not in the study. I saw this from the nursery landing. Nobody but Captain Voss was in the hall. I saw this from the nursery landing.

From when the joint was carried in until the guests had gone, I was in the dining-room. The hall was watched by nobody.

George Marlow's account. On Friday morning, Miss Pym said to me: "It was I who took your notes from the study." Miss Pym gave my notes to me.
