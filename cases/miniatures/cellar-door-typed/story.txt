THE CELLAR DOOR, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Martha Coyle, the keeper of the Anchor at Saltby. From ten o'clock on Friday night, the cellar door was locked. At six o'clock on Saturday morning, the cellar door was open. The best brandy cask was a gallon lighter. Who opened the cellar door in the night?

Ned Sharp is the ostler. At six o'clock on Saturday morning, Ned Sharp was drunk. Ned Sharp was in the yard. Jem Tully is the potman. At six o'clock on Saturday morning, Jem Tully was sober.

The cellar door was in the passage behind the taproom. Nobody but me and Jem Tully had a key of the cellar door. From ten o'clock on Friday night until six o'clock on Saturday morning, I had the first key of the cellar door. Jem Tully had the second key of the cellar door.

Ned Sharp lives in the loft over the stable. The carrier's man told me: "From ten o'clock on Friday night until six o'clock on Saturday morning, Ned Sharp was in the loft over the stable."

From ten o'clock on Friday night until six o'clock on Saturday morning, Jem Tully was in the passage behind the taproom.

Later on Saturday, Jem Tully said to me: "It was I who unlocked and opened the cellar door." At midnight on Friday, Jem Tully sold a gallon of the brandy to the carrier's man. At dawn on Saturday, Jem Tully gave a mug of the brandy to Ned Sharp.
