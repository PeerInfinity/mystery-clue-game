THE LAST FERRY, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Josiah Crane, vicar of St. Olave's. After evensong on Sunday, the christening cup was at the vestry. At nine o'clock on Sunday, the christening cup was not at the vestry. The vestry door was locked, with its lock not forced. Somebody took the christening cup from the vestry. Who took the christening cup from the vestry?

Silas Bell is the carter. After evensong on Sunday, Silas Bell was at the lych-gate. Silas Bell had a sack. The gate lamp was not yet lit. Was the christening cup taken before the gate lamp was lit, or after the gate lamp was lit?

Tam Reed is the lighter of the gate lamp. Tam Reed told me: "When the gate lamp was lit, the christening cup was at the vestry. Amos Dunn was at the vestry. Amos Dunn locked the vestry door."

Silas Bell lives at the far bank. The ferry was the only boat on the water. Old Hobb told me: "From when the ferry cast off on Sunday until eight o'clock on Sunday, Silas Bell was at the ferry. When the gate lamp was lit, Silas Bell was at the ferry. From eight o'clock on Sunday until Monday morning, Silas Bell was at the far bank. The ferry was moored at the far bank."

Nobody but me and Amos Dunn had a key of the vestry door. From after evensong on Sunday until nine o'clock on Sunday, I had the first key of the vestry door.

On Monday, later, Amos Dunn said to me: "It was I who took the christening cup from the vestry." Amos Dunn said to me: "It was after the gate lamp was lit."
