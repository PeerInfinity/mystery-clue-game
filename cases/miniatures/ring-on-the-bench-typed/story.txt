THE RING ON THE BENCH, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Esther Vane, goldsmith, of Fetter Court. The bench was in the workroom. On Thursday night, the customer's ring was on the bench. On Friday morning, the customer's ring was not on the bench. Who or what took the customer's ring? On Thursday night, the door of the workroom was closed to a person and to anything small. I had the key of the workroom.

Tom Pike is my apprentice. On Thursday night, Tom Pike knew that the customer's ring was on the bench. The window of the workroom was closed to a person.

On Thursday night, the walls of the workroom were closed to a person and to anything small.

On Thursday night, the gold filings were in a heap. On Friday morning, the gold filings were scattered. On Thursday night, a draught crossed from the yard to the workroom.

Signor Belli is the organ-grinder. Signor Belli lives at the house across the yard. On Thursday night, Signor Belli's monkey was in the yard.

On Thursday night, the ventilating pane let anything small through. The ventilating pane was closed to a person.

On Thursday night, the cord of the blind was fastened to the roller of the blind. The roller of the blind was beside the ventilating pane.

On Thursday night, the cord of the blind reached the bench. The bench was fixed to the floor.

On Friday afternoon, the customer's ring was in the bedding of Signor Belli's monkey. Signor Belli said to me: "It was my monkey who took the customer's ring from the workroom." Signor Belli gave the customer's ring to me.
