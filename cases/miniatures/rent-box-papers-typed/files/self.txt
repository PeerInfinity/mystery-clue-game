MY OWN AFTERNOON, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

At one o'clock on Tuesday, Mr. Fenn went out by the front door. From one o'clock on Tuesday until four o'clock on Tuesday, the front door was locked. I had the key of the front door.

From one o'clock on Tuesday until three o'clock on Tuesday, I was in the kitchen. The back door was shut. I saw this from the kitchen.
