THE PRIZE MARROW, TOLD FROM ITS DATA

I am Hester Vane, secretary of the flower show at Combe Regis. Abel Crouch's marrow was the cup's winner three years running. On Friday, after dark, somebody spoiled Abel Crouch's marrow at Abel Crouch's shed. On Saturday, Abel Crouch's marrow was kept off the show bench; by that George Pask gained the cup. Who spoiled Abel Crouch's marrow?

Abel Crouch's shed was without a lock. On Friday, after dark, Abel Crouch was at the allotments. Martha Crouch was at the allotments. George Pask was at the allotments. George Pask's marrow was second to Abel Crouch's marrow three years running. On Saturday, George Pask's marrow was the cup's winner.

On Monday, George Pask said to me: "I want my marrow to be the winner over Abel Crouch's marrow on the bench, and for that Abel Crouch's marrow must not be kept off the show bench."

On Wednesday, Martha Crouch said to me: "I want Abel Crouch's marrow to be the cup's winner this year, and for that Abel Crouch's marrow must not be kept off the show bench."

On Friday morning, Abel Crouch said to me: "I want my marrow to be kept off the show bench."

On Sunday, Abel Crouch said to me: "It was I who spoiled my marrow at my shed."
