## Inconsistencies in the text

- **x.race_day** — The race is 'next Tuesday', but the narration puts it 'four days later' than the Thursday of the investigation — a Monday. The time windows stay abstract (w.race_eve, w.race_day) so neither reading is baked in.
- **x.cup_plate** — The race is called the Wessex Cup throughout, but the race card names it the Wessex Plate.
- **x.bolted_amnesty** — Holmes tells Ross the horse 'bolted, and was cared for by one of your neighbours', though in s07 he says Brown deliberately hid it. The first is Holmes softening it for Ross, under the 'amnesty' he grants.
- **x.sitting_room_parlour** — The room where the pockets are examined is the 'sitting-room' / 'front room'; Ross and Gregory later wait in the 'parlour'. They may be the same room; kept as two places because the text never says so.
